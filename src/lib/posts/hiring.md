---

title: Who I'd like to hire
date: 2026-09-27
summary: A list of desirable personal characteristics. 
---

In no particular order:

1. The ability to entertain many and potentially conflicting ideas neutrally.
2. An interest in concise, frequent communication, and team synergy.
3. A desire to move/iterate fast and imperfectly. 
4. Comfort with discomfort or long hours.
5. The ability to admit technical errors while confidently standing ground on personal beliefs.
6. Interest and admiration for low-level technical details. 
7. The ability to have prolonged energy/enthusiasm/focus in a problem.
8. Being mission driven. 
9. Having a wide variety of interests (being interesting), i.e. good taste. 
10. Good writing ability.
